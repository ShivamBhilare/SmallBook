Based on "Cognitive_Load_Theory.pdf", here is a summary of reducing intrinsic load:

  • Simplify Information: Break complex tasks into smaller, manageable sub-tasks.
  • Provide Pre-training: Familiarize users with basic concepts before introducing complex ones.
  • Use Consistent Patterns: Leverage established design patterns so users don't have to learn new interactions.
