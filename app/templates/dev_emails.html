  1. Log in to your Supabase Dashboard and select your project.
  2. In the left sidebar, navigate to Authentication → Email Templates.
  3. Choose the template corresponding to your active tab (e.g. Confirm signup or Reset password).
  4. Click "Copy for Supabase" at the top right of this studio.
  5. Paste the copied HTML into the Message Body (HTML) textarea in Supabase and click Save.
